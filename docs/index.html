Policy Purpose & The Synthetic Envelope

This repository is a policy modeling tool, not a deployable field kit. The physical hardware stack (ESP32-P4, RTL-SDR receivers, SX1262 transceivers, PCB Gerbers, RF transmission firmware) is deliberately withheld. The software logic executes entirely on synthetic scenario vectors to model arms-race feedback loops and bystander collateral harms.

02 · policy claims: the arms-race dynamic

This demonstration communicates three core policy arguments to oversight committees and researchers:

  1. Claim 1: Surveillance pressure is generative. Community counter-surveillance systems are not the initial cause of distrust; they are the direct mathematical product of broad-spectrum, non-selective surveillance (such as warrantless stops, persistent aerial surveillance, and IMSI catchers).
  2. Claim 2: Counter-systems cause their own collateral harms. To build an anonymity set, deception engines (like GhostTrace) clone the wireless MAC identifiers of uninvolved bystanders. The counter-system pollutes commercial and law-enforcement databases with fraudulent bystander locations.
  3. Claim 3: The marginal cost threshold has collapsed. The accompanying $95 bill-of-materials demonstrates that commodity silicon (ESP32-P4, RTL-SDR, LoRa) has fallen below the cost threshold where counter-surveillance can be suppressed through equipment scarcity.

05 · intentional hardware withholding

To prevent operational weaponization while preserving the integrity of policy research:

  • No Firmware: ESP32-P4 and SX1262 transceiver firmware is omitted.
  • No RF Transmission: GhostTrace models mathematical decoy distributions, but contains zero code to transmit over the airwaves.
  • No Physical PCB Stack: Circuit board Gerbers, PoE hardware schematics, and enclosure designs are withheld.
  • Synthetic Data Only: All ADS-B DF17 transponder frames and EDACS logs are programmatically synthesized scenarios.
